The Making

Three gestures.
One mark.

01

Drawn

Before anything is cut, the monogram is drawn by hand. Every serif load-bearing. Every proportion deliberate. The drawing is the design.

02

Engraved

A steel die, guided by eye. One pass per letterform. No revision pass, no CNC correction. The hand’s pressure becomes part of the piece.

03

Crowned

Micro-pavé diamonds follow the engraved contour exactly. Each stone placed to the groove’s edge. The letter becomes the setting plan.
