The three-stone format is the oldest language in engagement jewelry. Past, present, future — three chapters worn as one piece. The design task was to make that language feel earned rather than inherited.

The oval was chosen for continuity. A shape with no hard corners, no point of termination. The flanking stones mirror the center proportionally — not identical supports, but two complementary replies in scale.

Rose gold because it doesn’t compete. The metal recedes into the stones. Every prong was drawn as thin as fabrication allows — the intention being that you see diamonds, not setting.
