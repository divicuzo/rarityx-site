18k Rose Gold · Oval Cluster · Past · Present · Future
I Past

The first stone carries what was. The years before the question was asked. Every version of the person you chose to love.

II Present

The center stone — always the largest — marks now. The weight of the moment. The heft you can feel on the finger when you look down.

III Future

The third stone looks forward. Open horizon. No design language can hold what that means — which is why it was left as a stone, not a word.

The Center Stone
Chosen Last

Rose gold because it doesn’t compete. The metal recedes into the stones. Every prong was drawn as thin as fabrication allows — the intention being that you see diamonds, not setting.

This is a pre-sold commission. Harmonia exists because the design came first, before stones were sourced or sizes decided. That sequence changes how the piece is made — and what it means when it arrives.
